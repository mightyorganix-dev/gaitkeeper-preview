FAQ

Good questions.

What does a website cost?

Most redesigns land between $1,500 and $3,000 per site. A lean redesign is $1,500–$2,500, a fuller one $2,000–$3,000, and two sites together $3,000–$5,000. You'll know the range before we start.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly we can trade notes. We'll set a realistic timeline together on the free preview call, before you commit to anything.

What if I don't like it?

You see your new design before you pay. If it isn't right, we change it, and you only commit when you love it.

Do you do signs and graphics?

Yes. Sign and graphic design is $120 per design, for signs, flyers and other graphics. You can see real sign work in the gallery.

Is there a sliding scale?

Yes. Small farms, growers and owners who can't afford standard rates: just ask.

Do I keep my domain?

Always. Your web address stays in your name, and old pages are redirected so you keep your Google ranking.
