Fence 6 · Meet Beth

Demanding, and always on your side.

Beth Bankemper rode as a nationally recognized "AA" junior and has coached ever since. A USHJA Certified Trainer with 35+ years in the sport, she shares her love of the horse with a select group of students at her home and farm in Franklin.

"My goal is to create excellent riders who know how to communicate with their horses."
